BIM and design tips

Model the room as a net clear area (inside faces of walls) when checking the 33 / 55 / 9 / 21 m² areas.
Check the clear height from the finished floor level to the underside of the slab or beams, not from the structural slab.
Keep wet areas, pipes and drains out of the room and out of the slab above; if unavoidable use the attic slab detail (clause G.7.4).
The 3D drawings show the checklist dimensions to scale for guidance only; room shape, equipment and trench routes are indicative. Areas and widths are minimums from the tables. Not every figure and table of Part G.7 is included (for example the ground-floor split transformer room table G.25 and the layout figures). Always verify against the full Dubai Building Code and DEWA requirements.

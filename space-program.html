BIM and design tips

Add a door swing check in your model views. The door must not open onto the trench and must be centered on the transformer base.
Check the clash and clearance zones (0.90 m and 1.8 m) around the transformer family.
Check the levels against the road level (0.00) before issuing.
The 3D drawings show the checklist dimensions to scale. Transformer size, trench route and equipment inside the rooms are indicative only. One line just above clause 4.1.25 in the source was cut off (apparently about a concrete protective casing) and is not included. Always verify against the full SEWA spec.
